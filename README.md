# Balcony / Patio Door Handle

A low-profile replacement handle for a sliding patio door, originally made by
Scheleger's and sadly now out of production. Modelled parametrically in
[OpenSCAD](https://openscad.org/).

## Dimensions

- **Total height:** 180 mm
- **Distance between screw centres:** 135 mm

## Printing

> ⚠️ **PSA:** I'm very new to this.

I printed a couple of drafts in very low-infill PLA and it was totally OK, but my
final print was in **PETG**, since this was for a spot constantly exposed to the
sun and lots of daily use.

I used PLA supports for a small part of the keyhole arc, but you might be able to
get away without them (haven't tried).

## Need to remix?

The original handle has a much higher, diagonal profile, to let you fully wrap
your hand around it. I made this one quite low because I needed to fit a fly
screen and the original handle was in the way.

The model is parametric — open `balcony_door_handle.scad` in OpenSCAD and adjust
the parameters at the top of the file (plate dimensions, handle profile, screw
and keyhole positions) to fit your own door.
